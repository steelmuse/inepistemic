Methodology

An inquiry that can criticize its own conditions.

The methodology of the inepistemic project: explicit commitments, human direction, and knowledge that returns to change the next question.

01

Methodological commitments

Make epistemic assumptions explicit enough to criticize+

Do not allow consequential assumptions about problems, knowledge, explanation, evidence, or criticism to disappear invisibly into system behavior.

Keep the human epistemically superordinate+

Artificial systems may generate, transform, retrieve, compare, and criticize candidate structures. They do not thereby inherit authority over which problem matters or which successor problem should govern the inquiry.

Permit criticism to transform the problem+

Do not constrain criticism to improving solutions inside a frame that criticism itself may defeat.

Let objective knowledge return+

Externalized artifacts should remain capable of entering later problem situations rather than existing only as terminal outputs.

Preserve enough ancestry for later criticism+

Do not allow the latest representation to erase the distinctions, unresolved burdens, or earlier criticisms on which it depends.

Make recursion criticizable+

When theory shapes architecture and architecture shapes inquiry, permit the results of that inquiry to return as criticism of the theory.

Do not mistake operation for validation+

A functioning system does not validate the grounding epistemology. Operational consequences create tests, not authority.

02

Recursive epistemic production

The architectural conjecture

If epistemic assumptions materially govern how a system processes knowledge, those assumptions should be explicit enough to become operational and criticizable.

Read the explanation+

inepistemic was produced by making selected epistemic commitments operational in a human–AI working environment, applying them to a live inquiry, and exposing them to criticism through the work that environment helped produce. Epistemology did not merely interpret the book afterward; it participated in the conditions of the book's creation.

When a conjecture shapes an operating environment, its consequences can expose ambiguities, contradictions, missing relations, unusable abstractions, or assumptions that survive in prose but fail in use. The methodology therefore treats operationalization as a form of criticism, not as validation of the epistemology.

The epistemic harness, Explanata, made selected commitments operative inside an agent environment. The AI model was not simply prompted to produce finished answers; it participated within a larger epistemic system whose governing structure was not supplied by the model alone. The Inventions page explains this implementation.

03

The human remains the epistemic operator

Read the explanation+

The creative person remains the problem originator, architect, critic, author, and selector of epistemic direction. The artificial system can obediently generate candidate explanations, variations, comparisons, retrievals, reconstructions, possible criticisms, successor problems, and inspectable structures. The human remains freely disobedient: able to reject proposals, alter criteria or framing, supply domain knowledge and criticism, judge explanatory progress, and redirect the inquiry toward a different problem.

This asymmetry is not determined by the proportion of text or artifacts produced by either participant. A machine may produce much of the visible material while the human participates across epistemology, technology, problem selection, reconstruction, criticism, and direction. Machine participation does not confer ownership of epistemic direction.

04

The Explainable Human

A supporting domain methodology, The Explainable Human, governed human-facing technology within the working environment. It is not a methodology for explaining human beings. It constrains how systems may observe, represent, classify, predict, assist, or otherwise interact with a person, opposing an epistemic inversion in which a necessarily incomplete system representation is promoted above the person represented.

Representations must remain provisional and criticizable. The human must retain the capacity to reject a framing, change a criterion, reformulate a problem, redirect an interaction, and, where the application permits, refuse or exit. An earlier form of The Explainable Human helped govern the environment in which inepistemic developed. The resulting book, in turn, offered a stronger account of the singular person as historically developing, open-ended, and capable of creating, criticizing, and reconstructing knowledge—an account that could be applied back to the methodology.

05

Criticism can transform the problem

Read the explanation+

The methodology does not assume that the original problem remains fixed. A failed explanation may expose a mistaken criterion, missing distinction, misleading abstraction, or even the wrong object of inquiry. Criticism can therefore produce a changed problem, rather than merely a better solution to the old one.

The familiar relation

P→TS→EE→P′P \rightarrow TS \rightarrow EE \rightarrow P'P→TS→EE→P′

maps problem, tentative solution, criticism or error elimination, and successor problem. It is not a rigid workflow or the methodological center; the essential commitment is that criticism may change what becomes worth asking next.

The book did not begin with a fully specified table of contents. Later problems emerged from earlier attempts, some sharpened, some displaced, and some only made visible by failure. The book's structure is partly a record of that problem trajectory, not merely a sequence of surviving conclusions. The Inventions page describes how the inquiry's changing problems were handled in the working environment.

06

Objective knowledge and explanatory ancestry

Read the explanation+

Inexplicit or partial explanatory structures can be externalized into objective artifacts that are inspectable, persistent, and available for reconstruction. Their methodological importance is not merely that they can be stored or retrieved. On returning to a later inquiry, an artifact can be criticized, rejected, extended, or reconstructed by a changing human participant; that encounter may change what the person notices, understands, or asks next, leading to further artifacts.

AI reduces the cost of generating, comparing, retrieving, and re-presenting such structures. The epistemic significance, however, lies in their return to a changing human problem situation, not in the quantity of outputs produced.

This return requires preservation of explanatory ancestry. If each new state overwrites the conditions that made it possible, earlier distinctions, failed conjectures, criticisms, and unresolved burdens disappear from later scrutiny. The requirement is not to keep every intermediate artifact, but to preserve enough structure to distinguish continuation, qualification, contradiction, extension, reopening, and transformation. Explanatory continuity concerns relations, not identical wording.

07

Recursive methodological criticism

Read the explanation+

The strongest recursion returns to the epistemology itself:

Et→At→use⁡(At)→criticism→Et+1E_t \rightarrow A_t \rightarrow \operatorname{use}(A_t) \rightarrow \text{criticism} \rightarrow E_{t+1}Et​→At​→use(At​)→criticism→Et+1​

Here EtE_tEt​ is the current epistemic framework and AtA_tAt​ an architecture shaped by it. The architecture enters actual inquiry; its practical and explanatory consequences can reveal problems in the framework, the methodology, or the technology. Criticism can then inform revised epistemic conditions. This is operational recursion, not a claim that repeated application produces truth. A recursive system may preserve or amplify error. Its critical value arises because the same commitments acquire different, inspectable consequences at the levels of theory, architecture, inquiry, and artifact.

The book itself is one such artifact. It discusses problems and successor problems, explanation and criticism, objective and inexplicit knowledge, epistemic motion, tools and abstractions, and human–AI collaboration—structures that also participated in its production. This correspondence makes the book a possible source of criticism of the explanatory architecture through which it was created.

08

Relationship to the technology

Read the explanation+

Explanata participated in the inquiry that produced the book. Kinematic Data Modeling (KDM) was applied after the book was complete, creating a distinct, source-preserving structured representation through which further analysis could expose new criticisms. KDM was not a stage of the original production methodology. The Inventions page explains both systems, their different roles, and their operating mechanisms.
