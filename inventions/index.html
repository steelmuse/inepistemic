01

Producing the book

Explanata

Epistemology as an agent operating environment

Explanata is an epistemic harness that makes aspects of the epistemology operative in software. It introduces a new data primitive as bounded units of explanatory work, called explanata. These can be exposed to structural criticism through criteria such as consistency, coherence, rigidity, and causal structure. The aim is to make enough of the explanatory structure explicit such that changes, omissions, contradictions, or unresolved mechanisms can become criticizable.

How Explanata governed the inquiry+

The explanatory data (or explanata) can then be processed across explanatory states corresponding to the recurring epistemic structure:

P→TS→EE→P′P \rightarrow TS \rightarrow EE \rightarrow P'P→TS→EE→P′

where:

  • PPP is an active problem;
  • TSTSTS is a tentative solution;
  • EEEEEE is error elimination or criticism;
  • P′P'P′ is a successor problem.

The base harness provided one governing layer of the operative system. The epistemology was itself processed through the Explanata harness to produce a system epistemology, an explicit operating methodology derived from the epistemology. That methodology could then be configured within the harness, alongside a supporting domain methodology governing human-technology interactions, called The Explainable Human.

The relevant distinction was not whether an artificial model could describe the epistemology, but whether selected epistemic relations could become part of the operating conditions of the inquiry. In that sense, epistemology functioned not only as content available to the system, but as part of the environment through which problems, candidate explanations, criticism, and successor problems were pursued.

In the production of inepistemic, inquiry proceeded through successive sessions organized around changing problems. The human operator determined useful session boundaries and selected the successor problems carried into later inquiry. This sequence corresponded to the book's chapters. Explanata provided the governed environment in which candidate explanatory structures could be generated, externalized, retrieved, and reconstructed across that trajectory.

02

After the book was complete

Kinematic Data Modeling

Produced through Kinematic Data Modeling (KDM), the graph represents candidate explanatory structure across the numbered book chapters 00–30. It makes explanata, explanatory relations, causal pathways, candidate transitions, observations, and source evidence available as explicit machine-operable objects.

The central modeling distinction is:

state  −  topology  −  motion\textbf{state} \;-\; \textbf{topology} \;-\; \textbf{motion}state−topology−motion

State represents what explanatory objects are present.

Topology represents how explanatory objects are situated relative to one another.

Motion represents candidate change from one represented condition toward another.

Keeping these dimensions separate matters because a relationship is not the same thing as a transition, a causal pathway is not the same thing as an observed change, and a candidate structure is not the same thing as a fact.

How KDM represents and exposes explanatory structure+

From a human-readable book to an agent-operable surface

The book is written for a human reader. It relies on sequence, examples, delayed clarification, remembered context, rhetorical movement, and the reader's ability to reconstruct relationships across long stretches of prose.

After the book was produced, Kinematic Data Modeling (KDM) was used to create a complementary representation of the same underlying work for artificial agents.

KDM asks a different question:

How can explanatory structure in a human-readable source be made explicit enough for an artificial agent to operate over it without severing that structure from its provenance?

Its current representation of inepistemic is a provenance-preserving explanatory hypergraph.

Explanata as represented objects

Rather than reducing the book to isolated facts, entities, or sentences, KDM uses explanata as bounded units of explanatory work. Those explanata can participate in typed relationships such as support, qualification, extension, development, contrast, or reopening as a successor problem. Causal structures can also preserve distinct roles such as cause, mechanism, and effect.

The representation is source-relative. Its function is not to assert that a represented claim is independently true. Its function is to make explicit what explanatory structure the source contains, how that structure relates to other parts of the work, what remains uncertain, and where the source evidence can be recovered.

That provenance boundary is essential. Increased machine readability should not create an appearance of epistemic certainty stronger than the source warrants.

What the representation made visible

Once the book was represented as a structured explanatory surface, it became possible to inspect long-range relationships that are expensive to construct and error-prone if reconstructed by agents from linear prose alone.

Analyses over the KDM surfaced structures including:

  • the singular epistemic trajectory;
  • changing successor-problem possibilities;
  • state versus transition;
  • mutable problem topology;
  • recursive objective knowledge;
  • preservation and transformation of explanatory structure;
  • unresolved explanatory burdens carried across the work.

A comparison between an automated review conducted from the human-readable surface and a review conducted from the structured surface illustrates the applications. The same objective knowledge can support different kinds of reconstruction when exposed through different operational surfaces.

Within inepistemic, derivative objects are therefore its proceeds: structured observations, explanatory maps, analytical findings, and other artifacts that can be independently inspected and criticized.

Core architectural commitment

Make the epistemic bases of software explicit.+

This approach is consistent with a core architectural commitment, to make the epistemic bases of software explicit. Making epistemic commitments operational creates additional surfaces on which those commitments can fail, be criticized, or expose new problems. That continuity is one of the central technical features of inepistemic. The epistemology is not only discussed in the work. Selected parts of it are instantiated in technologies that participate in producing, representing, and criticizing the work itself.
