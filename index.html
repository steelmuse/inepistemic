Recursive Self-Improvement Invention

This project extends epistemology from a hidden influence into the primary architectural layer governing human–AI inquiry.

Selected epistemic commitments were made operational through Explanata, an agent harness that governs how AI participates in the creation, criticism, and reconstruction of knowledge.

The project subjected both the epistemology and its technological implementation to a sustained inquiry, recursively using both the epistemology and the relationship of technology to people as subjects of investigation.

A book was compiled from this long-duration test and became material for further representation, analysis, and criticism of the epistemology, its architecture, and the technologies involved.

Book

inepistemic asks what happens when an explanation of how knowledge grows is applied to the singular person, to you. Follow that inquiry through genetics, psychology, institutions, freedom, consciousness, technology, and AI, examining what these different ways of understanding people can explain, what they leave unresolved, and how the creation of new knowledge changes what becomes possible.
